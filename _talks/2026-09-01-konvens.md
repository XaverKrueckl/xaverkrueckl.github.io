Visited [KONVENS](https://konvens2026.uni-hamburg.de) 2026 (Konferenz zur Verarbeitung natürlicher Sprache), hosted at the University of Hamburg (14–17 September) under the theme *"Context Matters: NLP Beyond Text"*.
Presented our poster on cross-linguistic situation entity segmentation for discourse analysis in diachronic English and German text (LAW XX) and visited tracks on factuality, tokenization, linguistic representation, language models, evaluating their output and AI generated text.
